# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People exploring a 3D orrery at a desk, often in a dim room: students, space-curious visitors, and the author learning Three.js. They want to travel between bodies, read a few facts, and toggle view layers without fighting the UI.

## Product Purpose

An interactive model of the Sun, planets, and major moons. Success is effortless travel, clear orientation (where am I, what is this), and HUD chrome that reads as instrument, not website.

## Brand Personality

Observatory, brass, quiet. Warm instrument light over ink-dark space. Confident, not playful; scientific, not NASA-poster.

## Anti-references

- Generic space UI: neon cyan on black, HUD scanlines, NASA blue
- iOS white circle-icon toolbars
- Glassmorphism panels
- Game HUD ammo and minimap chrome
- SaaS dashboard cards

## Design Principles

- Chrome is brass and ink; the scene is the color.
- One accent (brass) for selection, values, and focus; everything else is quiet.
- Controls share one vocabulary: discs, hairlines, uppercase kickers.
- The tool disappears into looking at worlds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA on HUD text and controls. Keyboard-focusable travel and toggles. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Focus-visible rings in brass on ink.
